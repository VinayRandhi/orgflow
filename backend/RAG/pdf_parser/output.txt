[HEADER] Level: H2 Text: A. ATTENDANCE

[LIST] 
1. What is my attendance tracking policy?
    - Your attendance tracking policy will give you information on late arrival, work hours, missing swipes. You can refer to it at this link, https://technovert.keka.com/#/me/attendance
      
2. I have missed my swipe, can HR or my manager adjust it?
    - You can find the attendance tracking policy, for the number of missing swipes you are eligible for in a week. Beyond that limit, you cannot regularize the missing swipes and for HR to adjust it, you need to seek approval from your manager, and then the HR manager can adjust it accordingly.
      
3. What is the attendance cycle?
    - The attendance cycle is same as payroll cycle and starts from Previous month’s 26th to current month’s 25th.

4. What is the last day to get the leave days regularized?
    - Since the payroll cycle is same as attendance cycle and salary processing starts by 27th, you have to get all the approvals and missing swipes adjusted before 27th.

5. How are Work from Home requests approved?
    - The Work from Home requests get automatically approved in the Keka portal after an employee applies them. However, if you face any issues please contact the HR team immediately.

6. How to record my attendance if I go to Keka office?
    - In a case when you are visiting Keka on a frequent basis, you can request your manager to add you to Keka attendance tracking policy or if it is on adhoc/rare basis then you can accordingly request the HR to adjust them for the day.

7. What is the Week-off policy for trainees?

[HEADER] Level: H2 Text: B. LEAVE

11. What is my leave policy & leave accrual policy?

The leave policy can be checked at [https://technovert.keka.com/#/me/leaves](https://technovert.keka.com/#/me/leaves). By clicking on “Leave Policy Explanation” in the leaves section, you can see the accrual, application restrictions, leave restrictions, leave lapse etc.

12. What is the policy of work from home? How many am I eligible for?

Work from Home can be availed by all the employees apart from freshers or anyone with <2.5 years experience. In case you are eligible there will be a button that pops to the right of the screen in Attendance section [https://technovert.keka.com/#/me/attendance](https://technovert.keka.com/#/me/attendance). By default, WFH is eligible to be availed twice a month.

13. My Salary did not get credited?

There are couple of reasons why your salary did not get credited, like,

[LIST] 
a. You haven’t submitted your bank details in KEKA  
b. The details are not verified by HR  
c. The bank account is not activated  
d. You provided incorrect details  

Please talk to HR to know the exact reason, and to address the issue

14. How many missing swipes & late arrivals are allowed in a month?

The Missing swipes and late arrivals are mentioned exclusively in your Attendance Tracking Policy, it can be checked in the below link. [https://technovert.keka.com/#/me/attendance](https://technovert.keka.com/#/me/attendance).

15. My leave got deducted due to exceeding missing swipes? What should I do?

In case you have missed your swipes due to fault in the attendance machine, it will be automatically adjusted by machine reset.

In case it is because you have forgotten to swipe, then you can regularize yourself to the number of times as indicated in the attendance tracking policy assigned to you, beyond this you need to get in touch with your HR.

61. When will I get my Relieving documents & Form 16?
Relieving documents will be couriered to you 2 weeks from the “Last working date”. Form 16 will be mailed in the month of June in the upcoming Financial year. E.g. if your relieving is in Jan 2019, you’ll get your Form 16 by June 2019.

Post confirmation of your resignation, your manager will inform you on the Knowledge Transfer schedule to your successor.
